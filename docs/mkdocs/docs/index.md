## Introduction

Suitefish-CMS is a powerful and versatile content management system designed to empower both end-users and developers alike. Whether you're a business owner looking to streamline your online presence or a developer seeking robust backend functionalities, Suitefish-CMS has you covered.

You can extend the CMS Functionality by adding Modules or extensions out of the internal store or by uploading them manually if you obtained them at our store. Visit the official suitefish-cms website/store for more information at: [https://suitefish.com](https://suitefish.com).

## Features

Delivers the essential tools to effectively manage your site’s content, structure, and user roles—streamlined for simplicity, without unnecessary complexity. For a broad overview of its capabilities, refer to the feature list below.

- **Access Control** – user/group management with flexible permission controls
- **File Management** – upload, organize, tag, and search media assets
- **Page Manager** – editor for custom frontend/backend pages with access permissions
- **Objects and Workflows** – lightweight PHP tool for object creation, dependencies, and lifecycle
- **Extendability** – built-in store for modules; supports custom stores and manual .zip installs
- **AI Integration** – connects to APIs like OpenAI; AI Agents, Vision AI, and image generation
- **Multi-Language Support** – German, English, Spanish, Italian, French, Japanese, Chinese, Russian, Hindi, Portuguese, Korean, Turkish
- **Integrated Installer** – GUI installer plus Docker/script-based options
- **Integrated Updater** – GUI updater for core, modules, and database updates
- **Windows Software** – desktop app for managing/deploying via Suitefish store
- **Developer Friendly** – multi-site management, Bugfish Framework integration, debugging tools
